# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) (CC BY 4.0), in a shorter form.

## Our pledge

We want participation to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, whatever their age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, experience level, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation. Contributions in English and Vietnamese are equally welcome, and so is a beginner's question.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to video editing, agents or the command line.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person. Say what you tried and what happened.
- Credit other people's work and respect licenses. This project exists because others shared theirs.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes, personal attacks, or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Publishing someone else's private information without permission.
- Posting material you have no right to share, or asking others to help download content in ways the licenses and platform rules forbid.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and pull requests that break these rules, and may temporarily or permanently ban people who do so. To report a problem, use the *Report content* option on GitHub for the item concerned, or open a private security advisory on the repository and say it is a conduct report. Reports are handled in confidence.
